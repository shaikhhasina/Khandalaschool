Academic Programs

A structured learning path from Pre-Primary through the primary years, built on the state curriculum and strong classroom fundamentals.

A learning path for every stage

Programs offered at our school

Pre-Primary

Nursery, Jr. KG, Sr. KG — play-based learning that builds curiosity, language skills, and confidence before formal schooling begins.

Primary (Class I–V)

Strong foundations in Marathi, English, Mathematics, and Environmental Studies, with daily reading and activity-based lessons.

Co-Curricular Activities

Drawing, music, physical education, and cultural programs held through the week alongside regular academics.

Assessment & Progress

Regular unit tests and teacher-parent meetings to track each student's progress through the academic year.

Our Approach

State curriculum, taught with care

Classes follow the Maharashtra State Board syllabus, with instruction primarily in Marathi and English introduced progressively from the early years.

Class sizes are kept manageable so teachers can give individual attention, and every student receives free textbooks and mid-day meals as per government norms.

Academic Year Highlights

  • Two terms with periodic unit tests
  • Annual sports day and cultural gathering
  • Parent-teacher meetings each term
  • Special coaching for slow learners
