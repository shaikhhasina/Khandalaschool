Our Approach

State curriculum, taught with care

Classes follow the Maharashtra State Board syllabus, with instruction primarily in Marathi and English introduced progressively from the early years.

Class sizes are kept manageable so teachers can give individual attention, and every student receives free textbooks and mid-day meals as per government norms.

Academic Year Highlights

  • Two terms with periodic unit tests
  • Annual sports day and cultural gathering
  • Parent-teacher meetings each term
  • Special coaching for slow learners
