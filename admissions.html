Required documents for admission

Please keep the originals and one photocopy of each of the following ready at the time of admission. Our office staff will verify the originals and retain the photocopies.

  • 1
    Birth Certificate & Aadhar Card Copy Birth certificate and a photocopy of the student's Aadhar card, required for new admission in 1st Standard.
  • 2
    Parent's Aadhar Card Copy A photocopy of either parent's (or guardian's) Aadhar card.
  • 3
    Passport-Size Photo One recent passport-size photograph of the student.

Eligibility & Notes

Admission to 1st Standard requires the child to meet the minimum age criteria set by the Maharashtra State Education Department for the academic year. For admission to other classes, please bring the previous school's leaving certificate and last report card in addition to the documents above.

For any questions about eligibility or documents, please reach out before your campus visit — our contact details are on the Contact Us page.
