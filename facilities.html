Safety & Comfort

A safe, well-equipped campus

Our classrooms are bright and ventilated, with clean drinking water, separate toilets for boys and girls, and a dedicated playground for daily activity and sports.

Mid-day meals are served as per government norms, and the school premises are supervised throughout the day to ensure student safety.
