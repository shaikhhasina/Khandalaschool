About Our School

A Zilla Parishad primary school selected under the PM SHRI scheme to become a model of quality, inclusive education in Khandala.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, curious learners from Khandala who grow into responsible, capable citizens — through education that is joyful, inclusive, and rooted in values.

Our Mission

To deliver quality primary education supported by government schemes, modern teaching methods, and an environment where every child feels safe to learn and grow.

Key Highlights

  • ✓Selected under the PM SHRI (PM Schools for Rising India) scheme
  • ✓Free textbooks, uniforms, and mid-day meals for every student
  • ✓Digital classroom and ICT-enabled teaching in progress
  • ✓Active parent-teacher and school management committee
  • ✓Focus on sports, cultural activities, and co-curricular learning

Admissions

Join our school for the upcoming academic year.

  • Visit the School Office

    Meet the headmaster to enquire about seat availability for your child's class.

  • Submit Documents

    Provide the required documents for verification at the school office.

  • Confirm Admission

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Previous school's Transfer Certificate (if applicable)
  • Address Proof
  • Passport-size Photographs
